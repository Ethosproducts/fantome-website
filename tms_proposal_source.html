Detailed Technical Proposal

1. Overview

The Transportation Management System (TMS) is a comprehensive logistics and fleet management platform designed for end-to-end control of bulk material movement. It digitizes operations such as dispatch, tracking, billing, and reconciliation, ensuring transparency, accuracy, and operational efficiency within internal logistics workflows.

2. System Architecture

The system will follow a secure, internal enterprise-grade architecture for structured operations:

Frontend React.js

Dashboards for admin, transporter, operators.

Backend Node & Express

Trip processor and billing/invoice engines.

Database AWS RDS PgSQL

Relational storage for transactional logs.

Integration GPS / Maps APIs

Real-time vehicle and location tracking.

This architecture ensures secure internal usage, high reliability, and structured data flow across logistics operations.

3. Core Functional Modules (1 - 4)

🚚 3.1 Driver & Truck Management
  • Central registry for drivers and vehicles
  • Driver profiles (license, document validity tracking)
  • Truck details (registration, capacity, compliance)
  • Maintenance and insurance schedule tracking
🎟️ 3.2 Gatepass Management System
  • Digital gatepass for inbound / outbound vehicles
  • Approval workflow for dispatch authorization
  • Timestamp logging for entry / exit security tracking
  • Direct linkage to trip logs and vehicle records
📍 3.3 Material Loading & Dispatch
  • Real-time trip initiation and loading confirmation
  • GPS and mobile-based location tracking integration
  • Live status updates (in transit, delayed, completed)
  • Material quantity verification at dispatch points
📊 3.4 Trip Management & Reporting
  • Automated trip creation and digital closure
  • Truck-wise historical trip logging and analysis
  • Trip lifecycle tracking (start → transit → delivery)
  • Exception logs (delays, deviations, breakdowns)

3. Core Functional Modules (5 - 10)

🔗 3.5 PO & Trip Mapping System
  • Direct link between Purchase Orders & Trips
  • Validation of dispatched vs ordered quantities
  • Prevention of duplicate or mismatched assignments
💳 3.6 Expense Management
  • Fuel expense logging on a per-trip basis
  • Toll, maintenance, and miscellaneous costs tracking
  • Trip-level cost breakdown and approval workflow
📑 3.7 Client Invoice Generation
  • Automated invoice generation for completed trips
  • Configurable transporter/client rate card billing logic
  • Trip-validated billing inputs to eliminate discrepancies
🤝 3.8 Transporter Management
  • Transporter and logistics vendor database
  • Contract rates, agreements, and validity storage
  • Performance tracking scorecards of transport partners
🔍 3.9 Transporter Bill Validation
  • Automated cross-checking of incoming transporter bills
  • Billing matching against digital trip logs & GPS logs
  • Immediate detection of overbilling or trip mismatches
📈 3.10 Reconciliation & Analytics
  • Financial reconciliation and cargo audits
  • Transporter SLA and performance scorecards
  • Trip efficiency and cost optimization reports
  • Operational KPI dashboard representing real-time movement
Enterprise Benefit: Combining PO mapping with automated invoice validation prevents leakages, eliminates manual transporter bill verification overhead, and ensures that paid weights precisely match loading gatepass logs.

Security & Deployment Strategy

4. Security & Control Layer

Industrial bulk logistics requires strict operational controls and financial audits at every stage of material transit:

🔑
Role-Based Access (RBAC)
Dedicated dashboard boundaries for administrators, transporters, and weighbridge operators.
📝
Immutable Audit Logs
Chronological history recording every dispatch modification, approval event, and billing edit.
🔒
Encrypted Storage & APIs
Secured API credentials and databases preventing unauthorized intercept of operations data.
✍️
Approval Workflows
Enforced electronic signatures/approvals for fuel requests, expense entries, and gate dispatch.

5. Deployment Strategy

The system will follow a private, intranet-first strategy designed for maximum business data isolation:

Internal Organizational Deployment
Backend and frontend services deployed on private company networks or controlled cloud server groups (no public-facing exposure).
AWS RDS Database Clustering
PostgreSQL database instance on AWS RDS configured with localized security groups, private VPC access, and rolling daily backup snapshot intervals.
Optional Offline-First Mobile Integration
Driver application supporting cached logs and offline syncing for remote highway zones with weak internet connectivity.
